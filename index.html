Parkpin AR saves a pin on your iPhone when you park, then guides you back with AR arrows and a live distance. A 2D compass takes over when AR is not available.

How it works

  1. 01

    Pin my car

    When you park, save your spot. You can add an optional photo, a note, or a level and stall number.

  2. 02

    Find my car

    Follow AR arrows and a live distance back to the pin. If AR is unavailable, a 2D compass is used instead.

  3. 03

    Back at the car

    The guide tells you when you are close or have arrived. The pin stays on this iPhone until you replace or delete it.

What it does

AR arrows

A floating arrow aims at your parked car while the distance updates as you walk.

Compass fallback

A 2D compass is used when AR is not supported, the camera is unavailable, the AR session cannot run, or you prefer it.

Saved on your iPhone

Pins, photos, and notes stay on the device. There is no account and no server.

Works offline

Parkpin AR does not need a network connection to save a pin or guide you back to your car.

Photo, note, and level

Add a picture of the stall, a short note, or a level and spot so the pin is easier to recognize, especially in a garage.

Built for iPhone

Designed for regular iPhones and the iPhone Duo. Requires iOS 27.1 or later.

Privacy

Location and the camera are used on your iPhone to pin your car and guide you back. Pins are stored on the device. Parkpin AR does not use an account or analytics.
