Parkpin AR

Pin the stall. Walk back to the car.

When you park, Parkpin AR saves a pin on this iPhone. Then it guides you back with AR arrows and a live distance. If AR isn’t available, a 2D compass is used instead.

Coming soon to iPhone

For iPhone and iPhone Duo. Requires iOS 27.1 or later.

What it does

What is in the app today. Nothing more.

  • Pin my car

    Saves this iPhone’s location. You can add a level or stall, a short note, and an optional photo of the spot.

  • Find my car

    Follow AR arrows and a live distance back to the pin. A 2D compass is used when AR is not supported, the camera is off, the AR session cannot run, or you prefer it.

  • Nearby, or arrived

    The guide tells you when you are nearby, and when you have arrived.

  • On this iPhone

    The pin, photo, and note stay on the device. There is no account and no server. Parkpin AR does not use a map service or analytics.

  • Works offline

    Pinning a spot and following the guide do not need a network connection.

  • In a garage

    If the location fix is weak, you can still save the pin. A level, stall, or photo is the practical way back to the right spot.

Privacy

Location and the camera are used on your iPhone to pin your car and guide you back. Location is requested when you pin or find your car, not when the app opens, and only while the app is in use. Pins are stored on the device. This website does not use cookies, analytics, or other trackers.
