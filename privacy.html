Privacy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Parkpin AR is a local iPhone app. It does not have an account system, and it does not use a server. This page describes what the app uses on your device. This website does not use cookies, analytics, or other trackers.

Location

Parkpin AR uses your location while the app is in use to save a parking pin and to guide you back to it. It asks for When In Use access the first time you pin or find your car, not when the app opens. It does not use Always or background location. Location is used on your iPhone and is not sent to a Parkpin AR server.

Camera

The camera is used for AR guidance and for an optional photo of your parking spot. If you decline camera access, you can still pin your car and follow the 2D compass, but you will not be able to take a spot photo or use AR guidance.

What is saved on your iPhone

The current pin is saved on this iPhone. That includes the coordinates, horizontal accuracy, time, and any note or level and spot you add. Optional photos are saved on the device as well. Your distance-unit preference and a setting to prefer the compass are saved in the app’s settings on this iPhone.

Parkpin AR does not create an account. It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking. It does not use a map service or a network server.

If you delete a pin, the pin and its photo are removed from the app’s storage. If you delete the app, iOS removes the app’s local data.
